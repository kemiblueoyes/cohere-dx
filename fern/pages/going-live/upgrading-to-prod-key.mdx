When you register, every Cohere user receives a free, rate-limited trial key. This page covers how to upgrade to a production key and what to expect if your use case requires safety review.

## How to get a production key

1. Go to the [API Keys page](https://dashboard.cohere.com/api-keys) in your dashboard and select **Get your Production key**.
2. Complete the Safety & Legal form.  You'll be asked to acknowledge Cohere's usage guidelines, terms of service, privacy policy, SaaS agreement, and model cards.
3. Indicate whether your use case involves any sensitive use cases outlined in the usage guidelines.
4. Add a payment method to activate your production key.

If your use case doesn't involve sensitive use cases, your production key is activated immediately. If it does, your key remains rate-limited at trial tier until Cohere's safety team completes a review within 72 business hours.

## Tracking incidents

The [Cohere status page](https://status.cohere.ai) shows system status, component health, incedent history, and scheduled maintenance. We recommend subscribing for updates with an email or phone number to receive notifications whenever Cohere creates, updates or resolves an incident.
